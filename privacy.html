Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026.

John Personal Google MCP is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It accesses Google data only when the owner asks it to, and only to complete that request.

When the owner makes a request, the data needed for it is passed to the AI assistant the owner uses (Claude, made by Anthropic) to carry out the request. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
